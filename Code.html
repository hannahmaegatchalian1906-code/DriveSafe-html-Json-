Help Center / FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a vehicle with DriveSafe?

You can book a vehicle by selecting your preferred car, choosing your pickup and return dates and locations, providing your details, and confirming your booking.

What documents do I need to rent a car?

You need a valid driver's license, a secondary government-issued ID, and a credit or debit card for the security deposit.

Are your vehicles insured and well-maintained?

Yes! All our vehicles undergo rigorous multi-point safety inspections before every rental and come with comprehensive basic insurance.
